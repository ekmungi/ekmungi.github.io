## Privacy policy for Claude MCP Connect - Google Workspace

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Claude MCP Connect - Google Workspace is a personal tool. Anant Vemuri built it for his own use, and only his own Google accounts are authorised to use it. It is not offered to anyone else.

### What the app accesses

The app connects Claude, an AI assistant that runs on Anant's computer, to his Google accounts. With his consent it can read and change his Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Contacts, Tasks and Meet data. It reads this data only when he asks Claude to complete a task that needs it.

### Where the data goes

- The sign-in tokens that Google issues are stored only on Anant's computer.
- The app runs on that computer and sends its requests directly to Google.
- The app has no server of its own. It does not sell Google data or share it with advertisers or data brokers.
- When Claude reads Google data to complete a task, that content is processed by Anthropic, the company that provides Claude, under Anthropic's terms.

### Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). Once access is removed, the app can no longer read any Google data.
