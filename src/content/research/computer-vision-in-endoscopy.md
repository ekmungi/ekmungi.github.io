## The problem

The aim was context-aware guidance for clinicians during endoscopy, such as detecting lesions. At Pentax Medical, the starting point was the unmet needs of users, which I identified with clinicians and key opinion leaders in interviews and black-box evaluation sessions.

## What was done

At the German Cancer Research Center I designed and trained machine-learning models for polyp detection and, as the only author, wrote a survey of computer vision and machine learning in gastrointestinal endoscopy.

At Pentax Medical, from 2020 to 2023, I led the early-stage development of AI-assisted detection and navigation tools for gastrointestinal and pulmonology procedures. Each concept was carried through product specifications to a working proof-of-concept prototype. Examples are image-based guidance in pulmonology and the detection of when an endoscopic ultrasound procedure is complete. Three validated innovations were transferred into product development pipelines.
